Our Technology

CrowdSafe AI utilizes a dual-stream convolutional neural network that processes both RGB and HSV color spaces to enhance detection accuracy. The model has been trained on a diverse dataset of crowd scenarios to identify potentially dangerous situations quickly and accurately.

For video analysis, our system processes multiple frames per second and provides detailed breakdowns of detected issues over time, allowing security personnel to pinpoint exactly when and where problems occur.
