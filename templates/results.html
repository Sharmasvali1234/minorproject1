Understanding the Results

Violence Detection

Our system identifies physical altercations, aggressive behavior, weapons, and other indicators of violence in crowd settings.

Violence indicators include:

  • Physical confrontations between individuals
  • Aggressive gestures and postures
  • Presence of weapons or improvised weapons
  • Individuals in distress or being restrained

Stampede Detection

The system identifies dangerous crowd densities, uncontrolled movement patterns, and panic behavior that could lead to trampling.

Stampede indicators include:

  • Abnormally high crowd density
  • Rapid collective movement in one direction
  • Chaotic movement patterns
  • Individuals falling or being pushed down

Normal Activity

Normal activity is detected when crowd behavior follows expected patterns without concerning indicators.

Normal indicators include:

  • Appropriate spacing between individuals
  • Orderly movement and queueing
  • Absence of aggressive behaviors
  • Controlled entry and exit flows
